Frequently asked questions

Is SpeedWork free?

Yes. All 32 tools are free to use with no sign-up, no limits and no ads.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. Every tool runs in your browser with JavaScript, so your code, tokens, text and images are never sent to a server. Your last three snippets per tool are stored locally on your device and can be cleared at any time.

What does zero-mouse mean?

You can work almost entirely from the keyboard: paste anywhere with Ctrl+V, copy the result with Ctrl+C, search every tool with Ctrl+K, switch tools with Alt+1–8 and categories with Shift+1–4.
