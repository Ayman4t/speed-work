About the List Deduplicator

Paste a list with one item per line and get back only the unique entries, with spaces trimmed and blank lines removed. You can sort it A to Z, Z to A or numerically, ignore upper and lower case, and show how many times each item appeared. The summary line states how many rows went in and how many duplicates were removed.

Tip: Turn on Show counts before you delete anything. The items with the highest counts are often the ones worth checking for a data-entry problem, such as a customer who was imported twice.

List Deduplicator is free, needs no sign-up and runs entirely in your browser, so what you paste never leaves your device.

How to use it

  1. Paste or type your input, or press Ctrl+V anywhere on the page.
  2. Adjust the options. The result updates instantly.
  3. Copy the output with the Copy button or Ctrl+C.
