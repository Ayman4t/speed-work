About the Meta Tags / Open Graph Generator

Fill in a title, description, canonical URL and share image, and get the full set of head tags: the standard title and description, canonical link, robots directive, Open Graph tags for Facebook and LinkedIn, and Twitter card tags. The preview shows roughly how the page will look in search results and in a social share before you publish.

Tip: Keep titles around 60 characters and descriptions under 160 so search engines are less likely to cut them off. Use a 1200 by 630 pixel image to get the large preview card on social networks.

Meta Tags / Open Graph Generator is free, needs no sign-up and runs entirely in your browser, so what you paste never leaves your device.

How to use it

  1. Set the options with the controls; there is no text to paste for this tool.
  2. Adjust the options. The result updates instantly.
  3. Copy the output with the Copy button or Ctrl+C.
