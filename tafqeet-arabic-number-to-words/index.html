About the Tafqeet (Number to Words)

Tafqeet is the practice of writing an amount in words so it cannot be altered on a cheque, contract or invoice. Enter a figure and get the sentence in English and in Arabic, in the formal 'فقط … لا غير' form used in Egypt and the Gulf, for Egyptian pounds, US dollars or Saudi riyals. It handles amounts up to 999,999,999,999 and includes the fractional unit.

Tip: Type the decimals you want written out. An entry like 1250.75 produces the main currency and the 75 piastres or cents, while a whole number produces no fractional part.

Tafqeet (Number to Words) is free, needs no sign-up and runs entirely in your browser, so what you paste never leaves your device.
